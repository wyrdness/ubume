## Notes on Sources

**Historical Context**: Understanding ubume requires knowledge of historical maternal mortality rates and the real tragedy behind the folklore.

**Cultural Function**: Sources emphasize ubume's role as both warning tale and expression of cultural anxiety about maternal death.

**Compassion Theme**: Many sources highlight the importance of compassionate response to ubume as a moral lesson.

**Regional Variations**: Different regions have distinct ubume traditions, particularly the candy-buying versus baby-holding encounters.

**Modern Relevance**: While maternal mortality has decreased dramatically, ubume stories continue as cultural expressions of the enduring power of maternal love.
